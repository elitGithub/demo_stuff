# Asset provenance

The 12 stylist portraits and 36 hairstyle studies in `site/public/assets/seed/` are original vector illustrations authored for Strand & City on 2026-10-21. They depict fictional people and styles. They are illustrative demo artwork, not customer photography or evidence of professional work.

Each SVG is a local immutable application asset with an accessible title and description. No external imagery, fonts, scripts, licences or image services are required. Private visitor uploads are separate and must never replace these shared files.

The approved discovery mockup also uses original vector illustration. System fonts provide the application typography.
