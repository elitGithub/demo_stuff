## Shared: FAQ

**Is this a template?**
No. It is a working system I built and tested, customized for your business: your name, branding, services, photos and texts, with features you did not buy removed.

**Who owns it?**
You get a permanent license to use and change your copy for your own business, and you own all your business data. The base system remains my work, which is how I can deliver this fast and at this price.

**What about hosting?**
Hosting is paid by you and stays in your name. I install the system on it. If you do not have hosting yet, I will recommend suitable options. If you would rather I run hosting for you, ask about my managed plan.

**What in the demo is simulated?**
Demos use fictional businesses. Emails, SMS and payments are simulated or switched off in the demos. Real email through your own mail provider is included; SMS and online payments are built for you as an add-on or as the Growth tier's integration.

**What happens after launch?**
You can keep it running and growing on a monthly plan: $500 per month for 10 hours of fixes and new features, with review of any breakage starting within 24 hours. Any bug my own changes cause is fixed at no extra cost.

**I need something the demo does not have.**
Tell me in the questionnaire. Small additions are add-ons; larger ones are quoted before work starts.
